What the exam looks like

From the two Fall 2026 mock exams posted on Brightspace. The real one follows the same template.

60 minutes, 6 questionsAbout 10 minutes each, 16 to 18 points each, 100 total. The mocks have 9 so you get extra practice.
Written, show your workPoints are given line by line (for example 1 pt for the mean, 2 for the SE). A right number with no work loses points.
Notes allowed, calculator notPrinted course materials and handwritten notes are allowed. Numbers are chosen to work by hand: √64 = 8, and values like e−1 = 0.368 are given.
Fill-in-the-blank PythonTwo or three questions give you a function with blanks: loops, lists, dicts, lambda, map, filter, reduce, comprehensions.
Use 68–95–99.7p-values and probabilities are estimated with the empirical rule or a small z table printed on the exam (z = 1.5, 2, 2.5 for 90, 95, 99%).
Scope: Weeks 1–4Python, data structures, higher-order functions, histograms, distributions, CLT, hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, regression, ridge, cross-validation. Clustering, kNN and naive Bayes (Weeks 5–7) did not appear on either mock.
