What the final looks like

From the CMPE Practice Quiz and the CMPE Final settings on Brightspace.

One attempt, Fri Oct 16The CMPE Final opens on Brightspace in class (1:30–2:30 PM Eastern). The practice quiz closes at 1:00 PM that day.
30 questions, one per page, shuffledThe practice quiz is 30 one-point questions, one per page, and you can't go back after leaving a page. A second attempt had the exact same 30 questions and the same answer choices, only in a different order. So there's no bigger hidden pool: these 30 are it. The BSEE Practice Quiz shares 23 of them word for word, which suggests both finals draw on this same set.
Wording and order will changeThe instructor's note says the final changes the wording, the answer choices and their order. Learn the fact behind each answer, not the position of the right option.
Watch "not" and "choose all"Several questions ask which item is not true, and several are "choose all that apply" with more than one right answer. Read the stem twice.
Beyond the 30The quiz instructions also say to know degree requirements, experiential learning and ECE policies. The extra questions here come from your earlier Brightspace quizzes on those topics.

How to use this

  1. Skim the fact sheet. Every answer on one page, grouped by topic.
  2. Practice the originals. In Practice, pick "Practice quiz (original)". Answers are checked when you click; "choose all" questions get a Check button.
  3. Then the reworded set. Same facts, asked a different way: flipped to "not", turned into true/false, or put in a short scenario.
  4. Take the mock final. 30 random questions, one at a time, no going back. Score and review at the end.

Terms you'll see

Advising and degree words the questions assume you know.

CMPE / BSCmpE
Computer Engineering, the degree (Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering). BSEE is Electrical Engineering.
Core courses
The required ECE courses every CMPE student takes, like ECE 20001, 20002, 26400, 27000, 36800.
CMPE Selectives
A list of ECE courses you pick from. You need 16 credits of them.
Special Content
Project-type credit that can count as CMPE Selective credit, capped at 6 credits: EPICS, VIP, Undergraduate Research (ECE 29600/39600/49600) and ECE 20008.
Complementary Selective
A broader elective bucket. An ECE course that isn't on the CMPE Selectives list and is below the 40000 level lands here.
EPICS
Engineering Projects in Community Service: team projects for real community partners.
VIP
Vertically Integrated Projects: multi-year research teams mixing sophomores to seniors.
Experiential learning
Learning by doing real projects. In the classroom: EPICS, VIP, research, senior design. Outside: co-ops and internships.
Concentration
An optional focus area on your transcript (like Software Engineering or AI/ML), earned by choosing specific courses.
P/NP
Pass/No Pass grade mode: you get "Pass" (C- or better) instead of a letter grade. Only some requirements allow it.
SEP
Student Educational Planner in MyPurduePlan: your semester-by-semester plan to graduation.
CRF / SRP
Steps in your advisor's registration process for next semester. They're listed in your advisor's Brightspace advising community.
API
Academic Progress Initiative: ECE's support program for students on academic notice (GPA below 2.0). It starts with a required meeting with your advisor.
CODO
Change of Degree Objective: switching into (or out of) a major.
Drop-in
Walk-in advising for quick things like add/drop or a grade mode change. Registration and API meetings need an appointment through BoilerConnect.
CCO
Center for Career Opportunities; myCCO is its job and interview site.
4+1 / BS-MS
Taking graduate (50000-level) courses as an undergrad that also count toward an ECE master's.

Not your degree. In Practice, set Major to BSEE to drill them. They're left out of the mock final.

The BSEE Practice Quiz has 30 questions too. 23 are word for word the same as the CMPE ones, so they're already in Practice. The other 7 are BSEE versions of CMPE questions.
